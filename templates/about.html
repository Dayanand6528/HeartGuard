Our Core Motive

Cardiovascular diseases (CVDs) remain the leading cause of death globally. Early diagnosis and proactive risk assessment play a decisive role in preventing acute myocardial infarctions (heart attacks).

HeartGuard combines standardized medical benchmarks with advanced evaluation algorithms to analyze clinical reports in seconds. Built to guarantee total privacy and fast performance.

Clinical Parameters & Feature Dictionary

Age & Sex

Demographic risk factors. Men statistically face earlier onset, while risk increases significantly for both genders after age 50.

Chest Pain Type (cp)

Categorized into Typical Angina (substernal pressure), Atypical Angina, Non-anginal pain, and Asymptomatic (silent ischemia).

Resting Blood Pressure (trestbps)

Measured in mm Hg upon hospital admission. Values exceeding 140 mm Hg indicate Stage 2 hypertension.

Serum Cholesterol (chol)

Serum cholesterol in mg/dl. High LDL levels lead to arterial plaque accumulation (atherosclerosis).

Resting ECG (restecg)

Baseline electrical activity showing ST-T wave abnormalities or signs of left ventricular hypertrophy.

Max Heart Rate & ST Depression

Maximum heart rate achieved during stress testing (thalach) and exercise-induced ST segment depression (oldpeak).
